# Design: VLA inference at >= 1 Hz on openTPU (F2): one slice scaled up with a packed int8 MXU

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-10-16
Branch: vla
Repo: Ergodex-Core/openTPU
Status: APPROVED
Mode: Builder

## Problem Statement

openTPU on the AWS F2 (VU47P) is an LLM-decode design: one slice with a 4-column MXU
(512 int8 MAC/cycle, ~0.1 TMAC/s at 200 MHz), 8 VPU lanes, 256 KB of TMEM, one weight chunk a
cycle from HBM. Decode is port-bound and runs Gemma-3-1B int8 at 22.3 tok/s. A vision-language-
action model is the opposite workload: π0.5 pushes ~560 valid prefix rows (two cameras plus the
prompt) through a 2B Gemma and 27 SigLIP layers once per action chunk, 1.47 T MAC, then ten
50-row steps of a 300M action expert. Measured on the ISA simulator with Verilator calibration
(docs/pi05.md 5), one π0.5 chunk takes ~15-22 s today: the MXU is the bound at 14.5 s, with
port B at 7 s (TMEM caps the prefix's row group at 8 rows, so the 2B's weights stream 70 times),
the VPU at 2 s. RhinoVLA (Qwen3-VL-2B at 64 visual tokens a view, a 0.4B expert) is 0.66 T MAC
a chunk by shape, ~6.6 s of MXU time today.

The goal: an openTPU variant (`f2-vla`) that runs RhinoVLA at >= 1 Hz per action chunk on one
F2 slice, measured on the board, with π0.5 at ~0.5 Hz, so that openTPU has its first compute-
bound workload and the levers LLM prefill needs too.

## What Makes This Cool

The 1 Hz number hangs on one arithmetic fact the FPGA literature says is false: Xilinx's INT8
white paper and every packing paper since guarantee only 7 accumulated terms per packed DSP48E2
because they assume the full int8 range. openTPU's quantizer never emits -128, so a 19-bit lane
holds sixteen 127^2 products (258,064 < 2^18) with a one-bit borrow fix-up at the chain end,
exactly the MXU's 16-deep cascade. Verified bit-exact in Verilator today (tests/test_mxu_pk.py:
9 tests, 0 mismatches, K to 16384), with the two patterns that break it pinned (sixteen
(-128)^2 in a lane; w_b = -128 beside a negative w_a wrapping the 27-bit pre-adder). That halves
the DSPs per logical column (137 -> 73) and makes 64 logical columns fit in 4.9K of the part's
9,024 DSPs, with the ACT RAM at the physical width (480 URAM at ACT_BLOCKS 128, the memory
budget below) because the packing is of weight rows, not activation rows. The same slice then runs a 2B VLM prefix at 8192 MAC/cycle and still decodes
LLMs through the wide port B built on the `wide-port` branch (off `ternary-bonsai`).

## Constraints

* Verilator first; FPGA synthesis only in the nightly build; no board runs on the shared F2
  instance outside the nightly's AFI. Numbers in the plan are ISA-tally or Verilator unless
  marked board-measured.
* VLA work stays in worktree openTPU-vla (branch `vla`; the packed MXU integration on
  `vla-pack`), never mixed with the Gemma / Bonsai work on `ternary-bonsai`. The wide port B
  lanes (PORTB 2) are built once on the `wide-port` branch, which is off `ternary-bonsai`,
  and merged into `f2-vla` as a named dependency.
* One slice scaled up, not several: no per-layer collectives, no sharding of π0.5's single KV
  head, no per-slice copies of the vision tower.
* The ISA stays: MM's row count is 8 bits, so ACT_ROWS is 255, which reaches 256's pass count
  (ceil(558 / 255) = 3). TMEM 1.5M words is what the 255-row prefix group needs (at 1M the
  compiler stops: "TMEM exhausted").
* 200 MHz is the target; the core clock is min(200 MHz, the packed column's fmax), kept at
  PACK 1 down to 180 MHz (the rule under Open Questions). 250 MHz is a nightly experiment with
  no number hanging on it (the only 250 MHz reference on this part, SGMV, is 20K LUT and one
  SLR; this design is 0.6-0.8M LUT across three).
* Accuracy: W8A8 moves π0.5's actions by 6.8% of their range, W8A16 on the 2B by 1.7%
  (docs/pi05.md 4); RhinoVLA's paper found W8A8 hurt success and shipped W8A16. int16
  activations do not pack (a 19-bit lane cannot hold 16 x 127 x 32767), so W8A16 layers run at
  one product a DSP: half the packed rate. **Precision of the >= 1 Hz measurement (Phase 8):
  W8A8 everywhere, with W8A16 only on the layer groups whose ISA-simulator error exceeds the
  bound in Success Criteria.** RhinoVLA MXU time a chunk at 64 logical columns, 200 MHz, by
  shape: W8A8 everywhere 0.53 s; the Qwen3-VL backbone at W8A16 (vision and expert W8A8)
  0.75 s (vision 0.19 + prefix 0.44 + expert 0.12); everything W8A16 0.90 s (Approach A's
  number: the packing then buys nothing). So 1 Hz survives a W8A16 backbone with the
  measured overlap only if the other units overlap well (serial sum 1.40 s); it does not
  survive W8A16 everywhere unless the overlap is near perfect. The precision map is built by
  a sweep (tools/pi05_sensitivity.py's method, `prefix_a_*`: one matrix group at W8A8 with
  the rest at W8A16, the chunk error attributed to that group), promoting groups to W8A16
  largest attributed error first, backbone groups before the tower and the expert, until the
  chunk is under the 2% bound.

## Premises (agreed)

1. The deliverable is RhinoVLA at >= 1 Hz on one F2 slice, board-measured, π0.5 at ~0.5 Hz,
   by scaling one slice up at 200 MHz. 1 Hz is an architecture demonstration, not a competitive
   controller (Jetson-class hardware reaches ~6 Hz on compact models with async scheduling);
   open-loop execution within a 30-50 action chunk carries the control rate.
2. On the model side the order is π0.5 (Phase 0 done) then RhinoVLA (phase 7), even though
   RhinoVLA's V3 branch ships no LIBERO weights (measuring its success rate needs a GPU
   finetune outside this plan). The hardware phases 1-6 (packed MXU, ACT RAM, lanes, TMEM,
   the fused unit, PORTB 2) do not depend on which model runs and proceed in parallel with
   the RhinoVLA port, in Verilator. "The hardware variant" means phase 8: the `f2-vla`
   target, its nightly synthesis, AFI and board run, which comes after the RhinoVLA port
   because that is what it measures.
3. Doing nothing leaves openTPU an LLM-decode design with an MXU no VLA prefix can use; the
   VLA variant's levers (packed MXU, wide ACT RAM, 64 lanes) are the ones LLM prefill needs.
4. The reusable code is named: rtl/exp/mx_mxu (the earlier packed DSP48E2 column), SGMV's
   lane structure for PORTB 2, the f2-hbm NCH shell, the Qwen3 kernels for RhinoVLA's backbone,
   π0.5's expert / flow loop for its action expert.
5. Distribution is the nightly AFI plus this repo; no separate release channel.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Minimal, MCOLS 32 unpacked
ACT_ROWS 255, LANES 64, TMEM 1.5M, PORTB 2, 200 MHz; no new MXU datapath. Shape tally:
RhinoVLA MXU 0.90 s a chunk, π0.5 MXU 1.90 s (~0.5 Hz). RhinoVLA stays above 1 Hz only if
the RTL's overlap factor (cycles / the slowest unit's) is <= 1.11x; the measured factors on
π0.5's cut phases are 1.10-1.54x. Rejected as the primary for that reason. It stays the
fallback, one build parameter away (PACK 0). **One rule for a missed 1 Hz, whatever the
cause** (the packed column closing below 180 MHz, the Phase 7 precision map projecting
under 1 Hz, or the board measuring under it): RhinoVLA is reported at its measured rate
(likely 0.7-0.9 Hz), Approach C is pulled forward so the action expert re-plans at ~5 Hz on
the same hardware, and two views instead of three (RhinoVLA vision MXU in A, 32 unpacked:
0.34 -> 0.23 s) is the model-side lever; 250 MHz is a nightly experiment, not a number
anything hangs on.

### Approach B: The plan, 32 physical columns packed to 64 logical (chosen)
As A plus the packed MXU (4,672 array DSPs + ~192 epilogue lanes ≈ 4.9K), the fused
activation unit after the lane work, 250 MHz as upside. π0.5 tally (ISA, ACT_ROWS 255,
LANES 64, TMEM 1.5M): MXU 0.95 s, port B 0.51, VPU 0.26, QACT 0.10, QST 0.21 (inside the
sum), DMA 0.06: serial 2.08 s; 0.5-1.05 Hz, ~0.7-0.9 Hz at the measured 1.10-1.54x overlap.
RhinoVLA by shape at W8A8 (tools/rhinovla_shape.py for MACs, MXU and port B; the VPU, QACT
and QST scaled from π0.5's by rows and vision layers): MXU 0.53 s, port B 0.25 (6.4 GB a
chunk at a chunk a cycle), VPU ~0.12, QACT ~0.04, QST ~0.21 (vision K / V stores; ~0.08 with
L11), DMA ~0.03: **serial 1.18 s** (0.85 Hz), bound 0.53 s (1.9 Hz); at the measured
1.10-1.54x overlap **1.2-1.7 Hz**. RhinoVLA stays above 1 Hz in B at any overlap factor
<= 1.89x, against 1.11x in A: that factor is the margin that chooses B. **The expected
Phase 8 case is the backbone at W8A16** (the paper's finding; π0.5's W8A8 backbone measured
6.8% against the 2% bound), unless Phase 7 shows RhinoVLA's backbone passes at W8A8: MXU
0.75 s, serial 1.40 s, margin 1.33x, **0.9-1.2 Hz** at the measured overlap. Vision MXU
figures by configuration: B, 64 logical: RhinoVLA 0.19 s, π0.5 0.14 s; A, 32 unpacked:
RhinoVLA 0.34 s.

### Approach C: Decoupled rates (deferred, not rejected)
Refresh the prefix KV at 0.5-1 Hz and re-step only the action expert on fresh robot state
several times a second: RhinoVLA's expert is 31 rows (1 state token + 30 action tokens), port-
bound at 0.012 s a step at 64 logical columns (tools/rhinovla_shape.py streams 0.31 GB a
step: 18 layers x (Q/K/V 4 M + O 2 M + MLP 9.4 M) = 0.28 B weights plus the projections; the
paper's 0.40 B counts its LoRA and embeddings), so ten steps are ~0.12 s, ~8 Hz; π0.5's is
50 rows at 0.016 s a step (the measured ISA tally: its 0.30 B of weights plus the step's
reads of the prefix KV and its own), ~6 Hz. Mostly software on A or B; the landscape's async schemes
(Jetson-PI, VLA-ULAP) do this. Deferred because stale-prefix accuracy is unmeasured and needs
a rollout harness, not the ISA simulator.

## Recommended Approach

B. The packing is exact today, its fallback is A by one parameter, and C is a software layer
on either once a rollout harness exists.

The variant `f2-vla`: S = 1, D = 128, MCOLS = 32 physical (64 logical, PACK 1), ACT_ROWS 255,
ACT_BLOCKS 128, TMEM_WORDS 1.5M, LANES 64, QST's V stores off the byte path (L11, below), NCH 8
HBM pseudo-channels, PORTB 2 (the expert step is port-bound at PORTB 1: 0.016 s port B against
0.014 s MXU), core 200 MHz. Peak 8192 MAC/cycle = 1.64 TMAC/s (2.05 at 250 MHz), 16x today.

L1-L11 are the levers of docs/vla_frequency_plan.md section 5. **L11** is the quantizer's
store path: QST writes a word a cycle for contiguous, word-aligned rows (the K stores,
confirmed on the RTL) but a byte a cycle for the transposed V stores (element stride = the
KV cache's column stride), 0.18 s of π0.5's vision tower at every configuration and the
tower's bound at 64 logical columns (0.18 against π0.5's 0.14 s of vision MXU; RhinoVLA's
~0.21 against 0.19); that 0.18-0.21 s is inside the serial sums above. Two mechanisms: V kept untransposed in DRAM (the PV MM reads V rows as
streamed weights: no new quantizer mode, if the MM's operand layout allows it), or a
transposed-store mode in the quantizer (a LANES x 4 byte transposer so each write is a word
of four rows' same column). Decision criterion: bit-exact with the ISA simulator on the
attention tests, and the tower's QST cycles below its MXU cycles in the tally; if both pass,
the default is V untransposed (no RTL in the quantizer). The agent on `vla` implements it.

**Memory budget** (VU47P: 2,016 RAMB36 = 70 Mb, 960 URAM = 270 Mb, three SLRs):
* TMEM 1.5M x 32-bit words = 6 MB = 48 Mb, banked by LANES 64: 24K words a bank = 768 Kb,
  3 URAM a bank: **192 URAM** (two-port, as today's TMEM pieces).
* ACT RAM: ACT_ROWS 255 x ACT_BLOCKS 128 x D 128 B = 4.2 MB (33 Mb) in all; each physical
  column holds the rows it serves (255 / 32 = 8 rows x 128 blocks = 1,024 entries of 1,024
  bits, read one entry a cycle). Width-bound: 1,024 bits a column = 15 URAM (72 b x 4,096
  deep, a quarter used) or 29 RAMB36 (36 b x 1,024 deep). **In URAM: 480 of 960; in BRAM:
  ~928 RAMB36** (the earlier "512 RAMB36" assumed ACT_BLOCKS 64, which caps K at 8,192 an
  MM: enough for RhinoVLA's 6,144-wide MLP, not π0.5's 16,384-wide GeGLU down without a
  second K pass). Phase 2 picks: the default is ACT RAM in URAM (480) with TMEM in URAM (192),
  672 of 960 URAM, leaving the BRAM for the FIFOs and the trace.
* Weight chunk FIFO: DEPTH 1,024 x 128 B = 128 KB: ~32 RAMB36 (the ACT RAM's K cap and the
  FIFO's replay rule 2 KB <= DEPTH both hold at KB 128).
* Result FIFO: 64 rows x 64 logical columns x 32 b = 16 KB: 4 RAMB36. Scale rows, the
  trace, the DMA line buffer: today's ~60 RAMB36.
* Per SLR: the floorplan sketch of phase 2 (32 columns across three SLRs, the TMEM banks
  beside their lanes); not derived here.

Phases (docs/vla_frequency_plan.md 7, each ending in ISA / Verilator results; the nightly
synthesizes the best point):
0. π0.5 port: done. RhinoVLA shape tally: done. QST's K stores confirmed a word a cycle on
   the RTL.
1. Packing feasibility: done in Verilator. Left: the packed and unpacked column out of
   context on the nightly host (`tools/mxu_pk_ooc.sh`): fmax is the open question.
2. ACT RAM at MCOLS 32 physical; a floorplan sketch per SLR (columns, result FIFO, drain).
   Exit: the ACT RAM bit-exact on the MXU tests at 32 physical columns with the URAM / BRAM
   choice and the per-SLR count recorded.
3. PACK 1 in the real MXU (in progress on `vla-pack`); MCOLS 8 -> 16 -> 32 physical with
   ACT_ROWS 255; RTL timing on the real prefix shape (558-968 rows, 16384-wide MLPs): MXU
   utilization >= 75% (in progress on `vla`).
4. LANES 64, TMEM 1.5M, QACT / QST wide: VPU + QACT + QST < 15% of the serial sum.
5. The fused activation unit, sized on phase 4's remainder (the GELU's ~0.14 s of VPU at
   LANES 64: ~6% of π0.5's serial sum, ~5-8% more Hz). The deliverable does not depend on it:
   it moves behind Phase 8 if the schedule is tight. Exit: bit-exact against an ISA
   definition of the fused op; the GELU's VPU share gone in the tally.
6. f2-hbm's 8 ports, PORTB 2 merged from `wide-port` (in progress there), the DMA
   path for activations: the expert step no longer port-bound.
7. RhinoVLA port (Qwen3-VL ViT new; Qwen3 LM kernels exist; expert / flow loop shared).
8. f2-vla target; nightly synthesis at 200 MHz, then 250; AFI; board run: >= 1 Hz RhinoVLA.
9. W8A16 where the int8 error shows. (A 96-logical-column stretch, 48 physical at ~7K DSP,
   would cut π0.5's MXU to ~0.63 s but not its 0.51 s of port B or ~0.6 s of other units,
   so it is not a route to π0.5 at 1 Hz and is not in this document.)

## Open Questions

* fmax of a packed physical column on the VU47P (DSP48E2 with AMULTSEL "AD", the 19-bit shifted
  D operand): the nightly's OOC run answers it. One rule (Next Step 1): **PACK 1 is kept if
  the packed column closes at >= 180 MHz; the core clock becomes min(200 MHz, its fmax)**
  (at 180 MHz RhinoVLA's MXU is 0.59 s, 1.1-1.5 Hz at the measured overlap, still above A's
  0.90 s at 200 MHz); below 180 MHz, PACK 0 at 200 MHz (Approach A, with its contingency).
* MXU utilization on the real prefix shape: the tally assumes every unit overlapped; the
  drain and the 32/64-row result FIFO are where a wide array loses efficiency.
* RhinoVLA's V3 weights: no LIBERO finetune exists; the >= 1 Hz measurement can use the
  pretrain checkpoint's compute shape, but success-rate parity needs a finetune on a GPU.
* L11's one open point: whether the PV MM's operand layout lets V stay untransposed in DRAM
  (the default); the criterion and the alternative are in the Recommended Approach.
* Whether W8A16 is needed on the whole 2B prefix for π0.5 (6.8% of the action range at W8A8)
  or a rollout shows it harmless: needs the LIBERO harness, not the ISA.

## Success Criteria

* Phase 1 exit: the packed column's OOC fmax on xcvu47p >= 180 MHz (WNS >= 0 at 5.55 ns;
  200 MHz, 5.0 ns, is the target); DSP 146 / column. Below 180 MHz: Approach A.
* Phase 3 exit: PACK 1 bit-exact with the ISA simulator on every existing MXU test; MXU
  utilization >= 75% on the real π0.5 prefix shape in Verilator.
* Phase 7 exit (RhinoVLA accuracy, before any board run): the fp32 numpy reference is the
  released `rhinovla_pretrain.ckpt` run as π0.5's reference was (tools/pi05_reference.py's
  method: synthetic LIBERO-shaped observations, seed 0, the noise passed in); the metric is
  max |a - a_ref| over the 30 x 72 action chunk divided by the action range, on the ISA
  simulator; **the bound is 2% of the range at the Phase 8 precision** (π0.5's W8A16 backbone
  measured 1.7%, its W8A8 6.8%): layer groups above it go to W8A16 until the chunk is under
  2%, and the resulting precision map is what Phase 8 measures.
* Phase 8 exit: RhinoVLA >= 1.0 Hz per action chunk measured on the F2 board (the nightly's
  AFI) at the Phase 7 precision map, π0.5 >= 0.5 Hz, actions within the Phase 7 bound.
* No regression on the LLM path, in Verilator (tools/perf_qwen.py's decode run) on the
  f2-vla build (MCOLS 32 physical, PACK 1, LANES 64, PORTB 2): Gemma-3-1B int8 decode cycles
  a token within 5% of the `wide-port` build's at the same PORTB (its projection: >= 38 tok/s
  at 200 MHz, docs/wide_port.md), tokens bit-exact with the ISA simulator.
