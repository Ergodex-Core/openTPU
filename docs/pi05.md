# π0.5 (lerobot/pi05_libero_finetuned_v044) on openTPU

Status (2026-10-16): Phase 0 of docs/vla_frequency_plan.md. The fp32 numpy reference matches
lerobot; the card's image and phase programs run a whole action chunk on the ISA simulator;
the int8 error against fp32 is measured, and so is the cost of a chunk, per phase and unit,
today and at scaled configurations (ISA tally), with three Verilator timing runs of cut
phases as its calibration. Nothing here ran on an FPGA.

Code: opentpu/llm/pi05.py (Spec, Weights, the host side, the fp32 reference, the quantization
emulation, Image / programs / run_chunk), opentpu/kernels/pi05.py (the Gemma phases; the
tower is kernels/vit.py's), tools/pi05_reference.py (lerobot's tensors), tools/pi05_check.py
(reference and ISA checks), tools/pi05_sensitivity.py (int8 points), tools/pi05_cost.py (the
tally), tools/pi05_rtl.py (Verilator timing of one phase), tests/test_pi05.py (a tiny random
model; seconds).

## 1. The model

A PaliGemma prefix and a flow-matching action expert, as lerobot's modeling_pi05.py runs it
(the module docstring of opentpu/llm/pi05.py has the details checked against that code):

* **vision**: SigLIP so400m/14 at 224: 256 patches a camera, 27 pre-LayerNorm blocks (1152
  wide, 16 heads of 72, MLP 4304, gelu_tanh), post LayerNorm, the projector 1152 -> 2048.
  Two real cameras; lerobot's third is empty (-1 pixels, masked).
* **prefix**: [camera 0, camera 1, camera 2, prompt tokens] with "Task: ..., State: <8 bins>;
  Action: " padded to 200: L = 968 rows of which Np = 512 + n_tok are valid (558 on the
  reference's prompt). The pads neither attend nor are attended and positions are cumsum(pad)
  - 1, so the card keeps only the valid rows: exact, and 42% fewer rows. Gemma 2B: 18 layers,
  2048 wide, 8 query heads on 1 KV head of 256, GeGLU 16384; bidirectional over the prefix.
  Only every layer's K / V is needed (the last layer's attention output and MLP are dead).
* **action expert**: Gemma 300M (18 layers, 1024 wide, GeGLU 4096, the same head layout), 10
  Euler steps from t = 1 (dt = -0.1) on x_t [50, 32]: action_in_proj, the layers with adaRMS
  norms (x rsqrt(mean x^2 + eps) (1 + scale) + shift, residuals gated), the 50 rows attending
  to the prefix KV of the layer and to each other, a final adaRMS, action_out_proj: v_t;
  x_t += dt v_t. The time condition (sinusoid, time MLP, silu) and every norm's (scale, shift,
  gate) depend on t alone: a fixed schedule.

## 3. The mapping onto the card (milestone 3)

One device program per phase; the host launches them in order (Image.runs, run_chunk). Every
weight is int8 (MX: per-row scales, power-of-two block offsets) in DRAM; each part's layers lie
in equal blocks, so a layer index (a run argument or a hardware loop's counter) addresses them.

| program | instructions | runs a chunk | work |
|---|---|---|---|
| v_embed | 15 | 2 | patches [256, 640] (the host's im2col) @ the patch matrix, + bias + positions |
| v_qkv / v_attend / v_out (v_last) | 420 / 316 / 274 (294) | 2 x 27 each | LayerNorm, q / k / v with biases, the int8 KV; attention; out + MLP (GELU-tanh); v_last adds the post LayerNorm |
| v_proj | 30 | 2 | the projector into the camera's 256 prefix rows |
| p_qkv / p_attend / p_out | 288 / 702 / 1586 | 18 / 18 / 17 | RMSNorm (1 + w), q / k / v, RoPE, K / V into the layer's cache; attention over Np keys (no mask: the pads are gone); x += O Wo^T, x += GeGLU |
| e_step | 2610 | 10 | one Euler step, the 18 layers in one hardware loop, the step a run argument |

* The host writes the patches, the prompt's embedded rows (tied table x sqrt(2048), fp32)
  and the noise; it reads x_t once at the end. Nothing else crosses between phases.
* **Time conditioning precomputed**: at build time the host computes, in fp32 as lerobot,
  every step's time MLP and every expert norm's 1 + scale, shift, gate (18 layers x 2 norms
  x 10 steps, plus the final norm's): the adaRMS table. e_step indexes it by its step
  argument, so the time MLPs and the modulation are exact fp32 (the sensitive pieces stay
  off the MXU), and the card does no per-step host work.
* **action_in_proj / action_out_proj in fp32 on the VPU** (io32, the default): x_t lives
  transposed in DRAM ([32, 50]); the in-projection is 32 OUTERs, the out-projection 32 RDOTs of
  the final adaRMS rows, then v_t + b and x_t += dt v_t per dim. 0.2 ms of VPU a step.
* The prefix KV of each layer is written once; the expert appends its 50 rows after the
  prefix's Np in the same cache every step (positions Np .. Np + 49), so attention is one
  pass over Np + 50 keys.
* GeGLU: gate and up rows interleaved by 256-row chunks at build time, so one MM yields a
  chunk's [R, gate | up]; gelu_tanh is 8 VOPs, then a MUL.
* Rows run in groups of R (tower), RP (prefix), RE (expert) inside hardware loops, so a
  program's size does not grow with the rows; the largest program (e_step) fits IMEM.

ISA simulator runs (OTPU_TARGET f2-hbm; the card's int8 everywhere except the fp32 pieces
above), against the fp32 reference of the same model:

* tests/test_pi05.py: a tiny random model (2 layers each, 3 steps): projector rows cosine >
  0.995, actions within 5% of their max (io32: 0.0041 of 4.0).
* cut model, 1 tower / 1 prefix / 1 expert layer, 2 steps (the real dimensions and weights):
  projector rows cosine >= 0.9999 (1.1% relative), actions 3.0% relative (0.095 of 3.1).
* full chunk: see 4 (FULL_ISA).

## 4. int8 accuracy against fp32 (milestone 3)

Measured on the real checkpoint and the reference observation: model-space (normalized)
actions over the 50 x 7 real dims, against the exact fp32 reference whose max |a| is 1.09.
tools/pi05_sensitivity.py emulates the card's quantization points in float64
(Emulated: the MX weight format, QACT's activation format, the attention's int8 q / K / V /
P), so each point can be switched alone; the tower's rows are lerobot's (exact).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **w8a8 (the card)** | W8A8 | W8A8 | **0.074** | **6.8%** | 0.0114 | .0060 .0023 .0143 .0003 .0047 .0008 .0061 |
| w8a8, io projections fp32 | W8A8 | W8A8 | 0.075 | 6.8% | 0.0125 | .0049 .0014 .0151 .0003 .0047 .0008 .0045 |

| **mixed** | W8A16 | W8A8 | **0.019** | **1.7%** | 0.0054 | .0036 .0008 .0067 .0003 .0012 .0002 .0043 |
| prefix_only | W8A8 | fp32 | 0.077 | 7.1% | 0.0111 | .0047 .0009 .0130 .0003 .0049 .0010 .0007 |
| expert_only | fp32 | W8A8 | 0.010 | 0.9% | 0.0032 | .0020 .0022 .0044 .0002 .0005 .0003 .0062 |

(Unnormalized: x the dataset's action std, 0.34-0.44 for the translation dims 0-2, 0.04-0.08
for rotation 3-5, 1.0 for the gripper 6.)

Reading it:
* **W8A8 as the card runs it costs ~7% of the action range** (0.074 normalized; up to 0.015
  in dim 2's unnormalized units). RhinoVLA's paper found W8A8 hurt task success; this
  measurement is consistent with that warning, and only a LIBERO rollout (not done) can say
  what 7% costs in success rate. Treat W8A8 for the 2B as **not validated**.
* The error is the **2B prefix's int8 activations**: the expert at W8A8 costs 0.9%, every
  attention operand int8 0.7%, int8 weights everywhere ~2% (w8a16). Of the prefix's operands
  the GeGLU input (gate / up: the RMSNorm output into 16384 columns) costs the most.
* The action in / out projections (already fp32), the time MLPs and the adaRMS modulation
  (fp32, precomputed on the host) are not where the error comes from: making the projections
  int8 changes nothing measurable.
* **Recommended numerics**: the prefix W8A16 (or at least its gate / up and q / k / v inputs
  wider), the expert W8A8: 1.7% (mixed). That needs an int16 activation path in the MXU (L9
  of the plan: half the packed int8 rate on those MMs, i.e. the 2B's). The tower is not in
  this table (its rows are lerobot's here); the ISA runs measure it with the rest.

## 5. The cost of a chunk (milestone 4)

tools/pi05_cost.py tallies every instruction a chunk executes (the programs compiled, loop
counts expanded, nothing run) at profile._describe's nominal rates: MXU N x KB x ceil(M /
MCOLS) cycles an MM; port B the D-byte (128 B) chunks of MMs and LD / ST; VPU rows x ceil(cols
/ LANES) a VOP, split by kind; QACT 2 ceil(n / LANES); QST ceil(n / LANES) + n (port A takes
a byte a cycle); DMA. Np = 558, 200 MHz, seconds. A chunk's time lies between the slowest
unit's (every unit overlapped: "bound") and the sum ("serial").

0.10-0.12 s more.)

* **ACT_ROWS alone does nothing**: TMEM (64K words) holds the row groups to 16 / 8 / 8, so
  ACT RAM's rows go unused; ACT_ROWS 128 needs TMEM with it (256K words: row groups 64 / 32 /
  32, port B 7.1 -> 2.3 s, the prefix's passes 70 -> 18).
* LANES scales the VPU and QACT linearly (2.0 -> 0.26 s at 64 lanes).
* **QST does not scale with LANES**: port A takes the int8 stores a word a cycle when the
  elements are contiguous and the rows word-aligned (otpu_quant's `wide`: the K stores), a
  byte a cycle otherwise (the transposed V stores: es the column stride); opentpu/profile.py
  models both since 2026-10-16 (before: a byte a cycle for every store, 0.29 s of the tower
  at every configuration; the RTL run of v_qkv, 2.196 M cycles against the MXU's 1.99 M,
  1.10x, with QST at 0.79 M under the word model, rules the byte model's 1.3 M out). That
  was 0.18 s of the tower at every configuration, the tower's bound at MCOLS 64 (0.18 s
  against 0.14 s of MXU). **QST T** (docs/isa.md QST, the same day: the V^T appends through
  the quantizer's four-row transposer, a word of four rows' bytes a cycle) brings the V
  stores to the K stores' rate: the table's QST column is now 0.091 s a chunk at every
  configuration (0.077 s of it the tower; the tally re-run with it), under the MXU at MCOLS
  64 (0.137 s). Within the tower's QKV phase it is still the longest unit at MCOLS 32 (5.3):
  K and V together are 64 port A words a token and head, against 36 MXU cycles at 32
  columns; past that the stores need the D-byte write path (plan L11's first option).
* **ACT_ROWS 255 is the ISA's most** (an MM's row count is an 8-bit field, isa.mm: 0 < m <
  256) and reaches 256's pass count over the prefix: ceil(558 / 255) = 3, as 256 would (4 at
  968 rows either way), so no ISA change is needed. Its 255-row prefix group needs TMEM 1.5M
  words (at 1M the compiler stops: "TMEM exhausted: a tile of 131070 words, 852282 of 1048576
  in use", and the group stays 128, which changes nothing). With it the prefix's port B falls
  0.40 -> 0.26 s and the chunk's 0.65 -> 0.51 s at MCOLS 64 (0.69 -> 0.55 at MCOLS 32); the
  MXU bound does not move. The tower's row group stays 128 (255 costs a little MXU time there:
  0.98 against 0.95 s bound).
* The MCOLS 64 point at 250 MHz: 0.76 s MXU (0.95 x 0.8), 0.52 s port B; with the RTL's
  overlap ratio (1.2-1.5 above, at MCOLS 4) **~0.9-1.2 s a chunk, ~0.8-1.1 Hz**, before the
  fused activation unit (L3: -0.13 s of GELU at 64 lanes) and W8A16 (which halves the packed
  rate on the 2B's MMs: +0.54 s MXU at 250 MHz if the whole prefix goes W8A16).

**The plan's >= 75% holds for one phase on these builds**: p_out, the prefix's MLP (two
thirds of the chunk's MACs), 81% at MCOLS 16 (1.23x; 68% at today's MCOLS 4, 60% at MCOLS
32); the other MXU-bound phases are 52-66% at MCOLS 16 (v_out, p_qkv, v_qkv with T, e_step)
and 35-60% at MCOLS 32, against 65-91% at MCOLS 4. Where the cycles go (`--profile`: a trace
run attributed by opentpu.profile):

* **The MXU drain losing TMEM arbitration, not the result FIFO's depth.** v_out at MCOLS
  16: the MXU has an MM in flight 96% of the cycles and pops 60%; it is *blocked* (chunks in
  its FIFO, no pop: the P/Q line's mb) for 738 K of 2.37 M cycles, and its drain loses the
  TMEM grant (fm) for 756 K, the same number: the drain's writes rank last in the slice's
  arbiter and the VPU (busy 48%) takes the banks, the result FIFO (64 rows) fills, the pops
  stop. At MCOLS 32 the same phase: pops 36%, blocked 1.04 M of 1.95 M, the drain denied
  1.05 M, the VPU busy 59%. e_step at MCOLS 16: pops 52%, blocked 195 K of 1.19 M, the drain
  denied 198 K (TMEMSTAT lose 197993 of the MXU's against 11606 of the quantizer's, 8179 of
  the VPU's). The MXU is never starved of weights (ms 0 and 1.9 K; port B busy 9-20%) and
  never waits for port A. **The loss follows the VPU's share of the time**: p_out's VPU (the
  GELU over 16384-wide rows) is 18% of its MXU cycles at MCOLS 16 and the MXU keeps 81%;
  37% at MCOLS 32 and the MXU keeps 60%; v_out's VPU is 51% / 101% and the MXU 60% / 36%.
  So 64 lanes (L4) would cut the loss about as much as the arbiter fix, and both are phase
  4's.
* **Dependency gaps**: v_out 161 K cycles in 5 gaps, 103 K of them on one QACT (the
  normalized rows into ACT RAM before the MLP's MM) and 51 K on an LD; e_step 89 K in 17
  gaps, on the QACTs between the attention's MMs.
* **The cut's head and tail**: e_step's prologue (before the first MM can run: the time
  embedding, adaRMS, QACT) and epilogue (after the last MM: the gate, the residual) are 139 K
  + 141 K = 24% of its cycles; the real e_step program has 18 layers in a row, so these are
  amortized there (without them the e_step ratio is ~1.46, the MXU ~68%). v_out's are 2%.
* **At 16 lanes the VPU is the longest unit at MCOLS 32** (v_out, p_attend, e_step): its
  cycles overlap the MXU's only in part (the drain's losses above are the other side of the
  same coin), so the MCOLS 32 ratios say as much about L4 as about the MXU. The tally at
  LANES 64 has the VPU at a quarter (e_step 0.112 M against 0.310 M of MXU).

So phase 3's exit criterion (MAC/cycle ~MCOLS x 128 at >= 75%) is met by the prefix's MLP
at MCOLS 16 and by nothing else; the two items in front of the rest are phase 4's: the
drain's place in the TMEM arbiter (or a write buffer in front of it: the drain writes E =
MCOLS / 2 words a step, a row in two cycles, and needs the grant for a fraction of the
cycles it is denied), and the 64 lanes. The ratios are honest about the memory path too:
port B is 9-20% busy and the MXU never starves, so at MCOLS 32 the weight stream is not the
limit at Np 558 (nor at Np 128 for p_out).

QST T, measured on v_qkv (the tower's K and V^T appends, 16 heads of 128 at 256 tokens): the
V stores from a byte a cycle to a word of four rows a cycle, the phase 1.306 -> 0.751 M
cycles at MCOLS 16 (-42%) and 1.265 -> 0.710 M at MCOLS 32 (-44%); the tally's QST 0.721
-> 0.360 M (K: 0.139 M wide, V: 0.147 M T, the rest the reads). p_qkv -8.5%. At MCOLS 32
